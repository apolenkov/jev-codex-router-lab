# Support

This is an experimental research lab maintained on a best-effort basis.

- Questions, ideas and bugs: open an [issue](https://github.com/apolenkov/jev-codex-router-lab/issues/new/choose).
- Security problems or an exposed credential: use a
  [private advisory](https://github.com/apolenkov/jev-codex-router-lab/security/advisories/new),
  never a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Before filing, read the [architecture](docs/architecture.md) and the
  [evidence status](docs/calibration.md); several limits are deliberate.

Never paste credentials, private data, or raw provider responses into an issue.
